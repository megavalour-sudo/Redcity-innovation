About RedCity

RedCity is a community-driven creative and technology organization dedicated to empowering youth through innovation, entrepreneurship, music, technology, and practical skills. We believe every talent deserves an opportunity to grow.

Mission

To transform communities by providing creative opportunities, technical skills, and innovative solutions.

Vision

To become Africa's leading urban innovation and creative hub.
